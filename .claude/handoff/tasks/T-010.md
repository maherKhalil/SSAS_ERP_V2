# T-010 — `ADR-028`: payment capture is tokenized, and `ADR-001` stands unqualified

- **Branch:** `agent/T-010-adr-tokenized-capture` — branch from `main`
- **Module:** Architecture (docs)
- **Issued:** pending T-015 merge
- **Depends on:** FP-014 (merged, PR #56)

## Goal

`OD-SUB-0016` ruled that the product issues invoices **and** captures payment itself. Read literally
as *the PAN enters the monolith*, that pulls every component sharing the process into PCI-DSS
assessment scope, and forces either a carved-out cardholder-data environment — in direct tension with
`ADR-001 Modular Monolith` — or a monolith assessed as one.

I have ruled it (`DEC-L-018`): **capture is tokenized. No cardholder data enters SSAS, ever.** This
task records that ruling as an ADR, because it constrains a whole module and the decisions log is not
where a constraint of that size belongs.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] A new ADR exists recording the ruling: the product owns invoicing, the payment flow, its state
      and its reconciliation; **the PAN, CVV, cardholder name and expiry are captured by the provider's
      hosted fields or redirect, never traverse any SSAS component, and are never stored.**
- [ ] It states explicitly that **`ADR-001` is not qualified and needs no exception**, and says why —
      the tension exists only under the non-tokenized reading, which this ADR forecloses.
- [ ] It carries the consequence a future package must respect: **no SSAS entity, DTO, request body,
      response body or log line may carry a field capable of holding cardholder data.** Cite FP-014's
      existing statements (`data-model.md`, `api-contracts.md`) as the first application, not as the rule.
- [ ] `status:` is **`Accepted`**, dated today, in the same front-matter shape as the other ADRs.
      This is a ruling that has been made, not a proposal.
- [ ] **The number is confirmed, not assumed.** `ADR-028` is referenced by `FP-010`'s README as a
      dependency and no such file exists — a dangling reference logged on this board since T-001.
      Establish what `ADR-028` was meant to be. **If it was reserved for V5 Document Management, take
      `ADR-029` and say so in the result**; do not silently claim a number another package expects.
- [ ] `docs/14-Engineering/ADR/README.md` lists the new ADR if it carries an index.

## Out of scope

- **Choosing a payment provider.** The ADR rules the *shape* — tokenized, hosted capture — not the
  vendor. Naming one would be a commercial decision I have not been given.
- FP-014's twelve files. They already carry the statements; this ADR is the authority they will cite,
  and the citation is added at ratification, not here.
- `Requirement-Numbering.md`, the master `Business-Rules.md`, `Product-Roadmap.md`. All at ratification.
- Any file under `src/` or `tests/`.

## Design constraints

- **Follow the ADR template and the shape of `ADR-027`**, the most recent one, including its front-matter
  keys and its Context / Decision / Consequences structure.
- **Do not restate PCI-DSS.** The ADR records a product decision and its consequence for this codebase.
  A summary of a standard we are deliberately staying outside the scope of is noise, and it will rot.
- **State what this does not decide**, in the FP-014 manner: provider, settlement, refunds, dunning and
  tax jurisdiction are all untouched by this ruling and each is unauthored today.
