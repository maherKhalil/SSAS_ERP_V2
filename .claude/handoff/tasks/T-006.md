## Goal

All seventeen `OD-SUB` carry rulings. The authority is
`.claude/handoff/notes/2026-08-25-fp-014-rulings.md` — **read it in full before the spec**, because
six rulings are the owner's, seven are mine, and four arrived in a second round.

This is the **first of three slices** over the 28 ruled requirements. It builds the model core only:
what the things are, how they are stored, and how they change over time. The authorization model, the
API contracts and the business rules are T-007; the acceptance criteria, test scenarios and
traceability matrix are T-008.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] `domain-model.md` names the aggregates, their invariants and their relationships, and is
      consistent with every ruling in the ruling set. In particular: the subscription is an
      **append-only history with exactly one record in force** (`OD-SUB-0008`), entitlement resolves
      as **plan ∪ additive grants with grants never lowering a cap** (`OD-SUB-0011` × `OD-SUB-0017`),
      and a **module** is the unit carrying one `IPermissionCatalogContributor` (`OD-SUB-0005`).
- [ ] `data-model.md` places every table in the **Platform database** per `DEC-SUB-0003` and
      `OD-SUB-0004`, carries no cross-database foreign key in either direction (`DEC-SUB-0009`), uses
      `decimal(19,4)` for money by inheritance from `ADR-027` without restating it as a new decision,
      and states explicitly that **no commercial table enters the Shared→Dedicated cutover inventory**
      (`DEC-SUB-0011`).
- [ ] `lifecycle-model.md` covers the subscription's states and transitions including **term and
      expiry** (`OD-SUB-0009` — expiry blocks login), and states the `TenantStatus` relationship as
      **orthogonal** (`OD-SUB-0010`): expiry never writes `TenantStatus`, and **no commercial reason
      is added to `TenantStatusChangeReason`**.
- [ ] Every one of the 28 `REQ-SUB` that this slice speaks to is **cited by identifier** in the
      document that covers it, so T-008's traceability matrix has something to chain to.
- [ ] `py scripts/trace-check.py FP-014-subscription` reports **no new ORPHAN and no contiguity break**
      introduced by these three files. It will still report the package incomplete — there is no
      traceability matrix yet — and that remains correct.

## Design constraints

- **Inherit, do not restate.** `ADR-027` money, `ADR-017` residency, `ADR-021`'s outage constraint,
  `FP-002`'s token exclusion. Each is already settled; citing them is right, re-deciding them is not.
- **The two directions of money stay apart.** The `README`'s boundary table is binding on this slice.
  Where you write "invoice" it means vendor→tenant, and the document must say so once, plainly.
- **A cap in force is a property of the subscription record live at that moment**, not of the tenant.
  This falls out of `OD-SUB-0008` and it is the detail most likely to be modelled wrongly — a metered
  overage is judged against the record in force when the usage occurred.
- **Follow the repository's aggregate conventions.** Read `Tenant` and `Employee` first. Value objects
  for codes and names, `Result<T>` factories, audit columns in the established shape, `RowVersion`.
  A novel shape is a defect even in a document.
- Where the ruling set is silent on a modelling detail, choose and **say you chose**, in the manner
  FP-013 used: the decision is visible rather than buried.
