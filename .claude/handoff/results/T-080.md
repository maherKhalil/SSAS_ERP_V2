## Every precedent verified. One ruling's reasoning was wrong; the ruling was right.

### `EmployeeImportRun.InvalidActor` — the two guards are NOT the same condition

The ruling rested on *"a precondition the handler has already enforced"*. It has not:

```
ImportEmployeesCommandHandler.cs:90-94   !string.IsNullOrWhiteSpace(currentUser.UserId)
EmployeeImportRun.cs:218                 !string.IsNullOrWhiteSpace(actor) && actor.Trim().Length <= 256
```

**The aggregate adds a 256-character limit the handler does not enforce**, so the handler does not
subsume it. What makes it unreachable is elsewhere entirely: `AuthenticationSubject.Create` caps a
subject at 256 as well. **Two equal constants in two modules, with nothing asserting they stay equal.**

**The 500 still stands, and for a better reason than the one given.** The actor is issued by the
platform and never chosen by the caller, so a caller cannot act on the answer whatever it says —
**and that remains true if the subject limit ever rises above 256 and the arm becomes reachable.** The
ruling was correct on an argument that would have expired; it is now recorded on one that will not.
