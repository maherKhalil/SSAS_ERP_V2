## Why the test needed a supplied store

**`CutoverConcurrencyConflict` cannot be produced on demand from outside.** It arises when a write-once
update loses its race and the re-read finds no observation — **not reachable by arranging fixture data.** So
the fixture gained one helper that runs the real fence over a real connection with a caller-supplied store,
and the failure is delivered at the fence, which is where the behaviour under test lives.

**The stub implements the two members the fence calls and throws from the other eight.** Returning empties
would mean a future fence that started calling one would be silently fenced against nothing — the shape
T-133 found in the unicode guard, where a green came from an instrument that inspected almost nothing.
