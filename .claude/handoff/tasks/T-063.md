## Part B — FP-009's eight cite a prefix that is not FP-009's

```
FP-009-hr-employee-import-export   ORPHAN TS-DOC-0020, 0021, 0022, 0023
                                   ORPHAN DEC-DOC-0011, 0012
                                   CONTIGUITY TS-DOC
                                   CONTIGUITY DEC-DOC
```

**`DOC` is FP-010's space** (`hr-employee-documents`), not FP-009's (`hr-employee-import-export`).
So FP-009 either cites its neighbour's identifiers, or owns a `DOC` space nobody registered, or the
two packages were split and the citations did not move.

**Establish which before touching anything.** The three have different remedies:

- **Cites a neighbour's identifiers** → those are `External refs`, which the checker already models,
  and the fix is declaring them rather than defining them.
- **Owns an unregistered space** → the owner registry is wrong, not the citations.
- **A split that left citations behind** → the identifiers moved and the references are stale, which
  is a documentation defect and the only one of the three where anything gets rewritten.

**Do not assume the third because it is the most interesting.** That reading makes the finding
largest, which is the direction that never feels like it needs checking.
