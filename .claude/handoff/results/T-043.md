## What shipped

`dotnet-ef` pinned to **8.0.0**, matching the EF Core packages in `Directory.Packages.props` exactly.
Without a manifest the tool resolved to whatever was installed **globally** — **10.0.2** here, two majors

resolved by the repository**, so every machine and CI agent runs the same one.

Pinned to the runtime's major version: a patch bump inside 8 is routine maintenance, and a major bump
must **follow** an EF Core upgrade rather than lead one.

## The premise was wrong, and the demonstration is the only reason it surfaced

T-041 reported six foreign-key operations emitted into a **data-only** migration — three
`DropForeignKey` and three `AddForeignKey` against `SubscriptionPlan`'s owned collections, restoring
`Restrict` in `Up` and setting **`Cascade` in `Down`** — and diagnosed it as tool-version skew.

**That diagnosis was mine, it was wrong, and the manifest does not fix it.**

| # | Hypothesis | Test | Result |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Tool-version skew (10.0.2 against an EF 8 model) | Scaffolded a throwaway probe with the pinned **8.0.0** tool | **Byte-identical diff.** Not it |
| 2 | The committed snapshot names owned types by their bare CLR name where EF 8 writes `Owner.Navigation#Type` | `ef migrations remove` regenerated those four lines; re-scaffolded against the corrected snapshot | **Still twelve operations.** Not it either |
| 3 | Our own model configuration | Read `PersistenceDbContext` | **This is it** |

**`PersistenceDbContext.OnModelCreating` ends by setting `DeleteBehavior.Restrict` on EVERY foreign key
in the model, ownership FKs included.** The migrations snapshot format **cannot serialise a delete
behaviour for an owned relationship** — ownership implies `Cascade` on rehydration — so the live model
says `Restrict`, the rehydrated snapshot says `Cascade`, and **the differ is correct.**

**It is self-regenerating and cannot be fixed by regenerating the snapshot**, because the snapshot cannot
express the value. Every migration scaffolded here carries the six operations until either the `Restrict`
loop stops touching ownership FKs, or they are committed once and accepted. **Queued as T-047**, which is
a design decision about a base context four modules depend on rather than a cleanup.

**"Phantom diff caused by tool skew" was wrong on both halves.** It is not phantom — the differ is right
— and it is not tool skew.

**I stopped and reported rather than finishing a task whose premise had just failed.** The architect ruled
