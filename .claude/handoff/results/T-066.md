## The dimension

`sequential` — 0001 upward, no gaps. `block` — themed blocks opening at round boundaries whose tails
are never filled. **Declared per package, printed every run, and a package in no entry fails.**

This is `DEC-L-061`'s fourth application, and the third time this exact shape has surfaced: after
`BRULE-` numbering and co-owned spaces, **a legacy convention the checker never knew, asserted against
as though it were a defect.**
