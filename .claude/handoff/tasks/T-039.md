# T-039 — Amend FP-014: expiry gates modules, and the trial is a plan with a term

- **Branch:** `agent/T-039-expiry-amendment` — branch from `main`
- **Module:** Platform (commercial plane)
- **Issued:** 2026-08-26
- **Depends on:** T-034 merge (PR #81)

## Goal

The owner has ruled the cutover, and one of the two rulings **amends a ruling already ratified**.
The record changes before any code is written from it — `DEC-L-027`, applied deliberately rather
than discovered afterwards.

**`DEC-L-033` amends `OD-SUB-0009`.** It ruled *"a term exists, and expiry blocks login."* It now
reads: **expiry gates modules and does not block login.** An expired tenant authenticates, reaches
the platform plane — account, users, and the subscription surface itself — and reaches no gated
module. The reasoning: a lapsed customer who cannot log in cannot reach the page that would let them
subscribe.

**`DEC-L-034` settles the trial**, and it needs no new concept: a tenant without a subscription gets
an **all-module plan with a 14-day term**, seeded at cutover for the existing estate and on tenant
creation thereafter. `OD-SUB-0014` already ruled *"a trial, if ever needed, is a plan with a short
term — not a state, not a flag."* This is that ruling being used, not overturned.

**Docs only. No `src/`, no `tests/`.** The resolver is T-040 and the seeding is T-041.
