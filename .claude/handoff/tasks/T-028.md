## Goal

You found the one rule of twenty-one that does not survive promotion, promoted it verbatim anyway
because my constraint was the stronger one, and refused to guess at a rewording. All three were right.

**The ruling: the product-wide reading is correct, and here is why it is not a guess.**
`BR-SUB-0020` was written in **T-007**. `ADR-029` was written in **T-010**, three tasks later, and it
rules that no cardholder datum enters **SSAS** — not the commercial plane, not this package, the
product — with `DEC-L-018` behind it. So *"by this package"* is an artefact of **which document existed
first**, not a deliberate narrowing. Nothing ever ruled the narrow scope; the wide one was ruled after
the sentence was written and the sentence was never revisited.

That is exactly the case where amending the source and re-promoting is right, and rewording in transit
would have been wrong.
