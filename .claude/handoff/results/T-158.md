## §4 — self-service grants: the mechanism is not what the item says

> *"Employee self-service is gated by **per-user permission grants**... Granting it to a workforce means
> granting it one person at a time."*

**There is no per-user permission grant in this product.** `TenantUser` holds
`TenantUserRoleAssignment` — a list of ROLES. There is no `TenantUserPermission` or `UserPermissionGrant`
entity anywhere in `src/`.

**Permissions reach a user only through a role.** So the listed option —

> *"**A bulk grant** — by role, department, or 'all active employees'."*

— **is already half-built: granting by role is the only mechanism that exists.** What is absent is a way to
assign a role to many users at once, or a role granted by default at hire.

**The three permissions are confirmed exactly as recorded** (`Attendance.Records.ViewOwn`,
`Attendance.Leave.ViewOwn`, `Payroll.Payslips.ViewOwn`), **and none of them appears in any seeded role.**
