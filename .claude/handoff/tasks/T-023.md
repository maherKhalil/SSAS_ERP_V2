- [ ] **Each of the five is tested individually against use**, and the evidence is named per ADR: the
      shipped code, the migration, the suite, the guard, or the approved package that relies on it.
      `ADR-020`'s cutover machinery and `ADR-022`'s backup/restore work are the obvious places to look;
      do not assume the other three are equally well evidenced.
- [ ] **Only what clears moves.** Each that moves gets `status: Accepted`, an evidence line saying
      **inference** in that word, a Revision History row in that file's own shape, and a version bump —
      the T-011/T-020 pattern exactly.
- [ ] **Anything whose evidence does not clear is left alone and reported.** Five moved is a suspicious
      result; three moved and two queried is a normal one. I am not ruling in advance that any of them
      should move.
- [ ] **`ADR-021` is reported on explicitly whatever the outcome**, because it is the one FP-014 rests
      on. If it clears, say what carried it. If it does not, say what it is waiting on — that becomes a
      `NEEDS-DECISION` for me, since a ratified package citing a `Proposed` ADR cannot simply stand.
- [ ] `docs/14-Engineering/ADR/README.md` reflects whatever moved, and
      `git diff main...agent/T-023-tenant-storage-adrs --stat` shows **at most six files**, all under
      `docs/14-Engineering/ADR/`.
