# T-050 — `DEC-L-049` option B: correct `ADR-018`'s procedure and the tool's false reassurance

- **Branch:** `agent/T-050-adr-018-procedure` — branch from `main`

- **Depends on:** T-048 (PR #97). **Non-gated.**

## Goal

You diagnosed it and I ruled it. This applies option B and nothing else.

`ADR-018:520`–`523` documents the tenant stream with `--project` **and** `--startup-project` both on
`src/Platform/SSAS.Platform.Infrastructure`. **Harmless for the `database update` it shows** — applying
runs recorded SQL rather than diffing a model — **and destructive for the `migrations add` beside it**,
which scaffolds 32 `DropTable` covering the entire module surface.

**The procedure is right for the command it shows and wrong for the one beside it, and nothing says so.**

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] **`ADR-018`'s tenant procedure distinguishes the two commands.** `database update` may keep the
      Platform startup project; **`migrations add` against `TenantDbContext` must use
      `tools/SSAS.Tenant.MigrationTool` as `--startup-project`**, and the ADR says which and why.
- [ ] **The reason is stated, not just the flag.** A reader who is told to use a different startup
      project without being told that the Platform one produces a destructive scaffold will substitute
      the familiar command the first time the tool is inconvenient.
- [ ] **The tool's comment is corrected — it is a false reassurance, not a stale one.**
      `ComposedTenantDbContextFactory.cs:19-20` says Platform's factory *"would silently produce an
      EMPTY migration for a module's tables."* **It produces a destructive one.** Empty and destructive
      call for opposite reactions, and a reader who remembers that sentence stops checking.
      **`DEC-L-039`:** it was written before anyone had tested it — date the correction rather than
      pretending the claim was never made.
- [ ] **Nothing else in `ADR-018` changes**, and **no code changes.** `TenantDbContextDesignTimeFactory`
      is correct for its scope and is not touched — that is the whole of `DEC-L-049`.
- [ ] `git diff --stat` shows **at most two files**: the ADR and the tool's factory comment.

  contributor-less model outright, **which would also break `database update`.** That gets established
  before it is adopted, and not tonight.
- `TenantDbContextDesignTimeFactory`, every migration, and `settings.json`.

## Design constraints

- **`ADR-018` is `Proposed`.** Do not change its status — that is `DEC-L-020`'s question and not this
  task's. If correcting the procedure makes its status look odder, **report it**.
- **Do not describe the defect as a tooling bug.** It is a documented procedure that is wrong for one
  of the two commands it sits beside. Naming it accurately is what stops someone "fixing" the factory.
- **`DEC-L-045` applies to the wording:** a procedure that merely omits the dangerous invocation leaves
  it available and unmarked. Say what goes wrong, so someone who does it anyway recognises the output.
