## Goal

`CON-0001` says the product **shall** operate as a subscription-based SaaS platform. `BR-PLT-0008`
says modules may be enabled or disabled per subscription plan and that disabled modules shall not
appear in menus or APIs. Neither has a requirement, a package, a roadmap entry, or a line of code,
and `CON-0001` appears exactly once in the entire `docs/` tree — in its own definition.

The owner has ruled that `CON-0001` is **upheld, not amended** (`DEC-L-004`), and that the commercial
plane gets a full analysis package rather than a narrow enablement fix.

This task builds the **foundation** of that package only: the sweep, the README, the requirements,
and the open-decision register. The model documents, the matrices and any code come later, and none
of them can be written until the owner has ruled on the register this task produces.

Read `.claude/handoff/notes/2026-08-25-saas-commercial-plane.md` first. It is the finding this
package answers, with the evidence already gathered — do not re-derive it, but **do re-verify it**
before you repeat any of it as fact.

## Design constraints

- **Do not conflate the two billing directions.** This package is about billing *the tenant* for
  their subscription to the product. The ERP's own General Ledger, and any future receivables module,
  are about the tenant billing *their* customers. These are different money, different ledgers,
  different authority, and different tenancy scope. Conflating them is the single most likely way
  this package goes wrong; say plainly which one is in scope.
- **The subscription plane is tenant-scoped but not tenant-owned data.** A tenant must not be able to
  edit its own plan. Where the record lives relative to `ADR-017`'s topology — platform database
  versus tenant database — is a consequence of that, and is an `OD-SUB`, not an assumption.
- **`BR-PLT-0008` is an enforcement rule, not a data rule.** Its clause is *menus **and** APIs*. Any
  requirement you write for it has to be checkable at the route surface, because
  `src/Host/SSAS.Host.API/Program.cs` currently mounts all twelve route groups unconditionally.
- **Do not presume pricing, currency, tax, proration, invoicing authority or payment capture.** Each
  is an `OD-SUB` with no default. `ADR-027` already fixes money representation at `decimal(19,4)` —
  inherit that, do not restate it as a new decision.
- **State what authority does not exist.** FP-013's package was strongest where it said plainly that
  the Glossary's "Leave Request" was an illustration and not a requirement. Do the same here: the
  authority for this package is three lines, and the package should say so rather than dress them up.
