## ⚠ AND THE FIRST INSTRUMENT I BUILT WAS WRONG, WHICH IS WHY I CHECKED IT BEFORE REPORTING

**My first dump reported nine routes with `(none)` for a policy, and I nearly reported them as unguarded.**
`IAuthorizeData.Policy` is **null for `.RequireAuthorization()` with no argument** — so *authorized with no
named policy* and *not authorized at all* read identically.

Re-measured against metadata PRESENCE rather than the policy string:

```
auth=1, no named policy   logout x2, effective x2       authenticated, deliberately no permission
auth=0, AllowAnonymous    login x2, refresh x2, select-tenant   anonymous by nature
```

**Every route is either gated, authenticated, or explicitly anonymous. There is no unguarded route in
Platform.** That is negative evidence and it is the honest headline for the guard dimension.

**Same class as T-126's near-miss, caught the same way** — the instrument checked before its output was
believed.
