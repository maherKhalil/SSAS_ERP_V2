**Placed in Version 1, immediately after Tenant Management.** Three reasons, in order of weight:

1. **`CON-0001` is a V1 constraint, not a future capability.** *"The application **shall** operate as a
   subscription-based SaaS platform."* A roadmap that runs to Version 5 without naming subscription
   anywhere was not deferring the capability — it had lost it.
2. **`BR-PLT-0008` is already a Platform rule in the master register**, and it is violated by every
   mounted route group today. The capability is **already owed**, not newly proposed.
3. **`OD-SUB-0003` ruled the enablement gate ships before the next module.** Any placement in V2 or
   later would contradict a ruling already made.

Within V1 it joins the `* Management` family after **Tenant Management**, because `OD-SUB-0010` ruled
subscription state and `TenantStatus` **orthogonal dimensions of the same subject**, both checked at
login. Placing it beside the tenant is where a reader will look for it.
