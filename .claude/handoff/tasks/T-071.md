# T-071 — What a `.self` permission would cost, established not designed

**Measurement, feeding FP-015's `authorization-model.md`. Build nothing.**

## Why

`OD-SS-0001` is ruled: self-service is authorised by a **distinct permission** — `payroll.payslip.view.self`
alongside `payroll.payslip.view` — because a scope applied at the handler relies on every handler
remembering, and **the architecture guards assert permissions, not scopes.**

**`REQ-ATT-0023` asserts that no `ViewOwn` permission exists.** This task establishes what creating
one actually involves, before `authorization-model.md` claims it is simple.

## Establish — with file and line, not a summary

**1. How a permission is defined today.** `PlatformPermissionCatalog`, `PlatformPermissionNames`,
`ModulePermissionDefinition`, and whatever else participates. **What must be edited, in how many
places, to add one permission?**

**2. What asserts the catalog is complete.** There are architecture guards over permissions; find
them and report **what they would demand of a `.self` permission** — naming, registration,
contribution, a seed. **A guard that fires on an incomplete addition is the useful finding**, because
it is the thing that would catch a half-built one.

**3. Whether `.self` fits the existing naming and scoping model.** `PermissionScope.PlatformSupport`
exists and is described as *"cross-tenant authority that is never tenant-assignable."* **Is there a
scope concept that a self permission would belong to, or would it be an ordinary tenant-assignable
permission that happens to end in `.self`?** These are materially different and the answer is in the
code, not in preference.

**4. Who grants it.** A permission nobody can assign is a permission nobody has. **Establish how a
tenant would grant `payroll.payslip.view.self` to every employee** — role, default, seed — and
whether that mechanism exists today.

## The question behind all four

**`AC-SS-0006` says the self permission must not imply the administrative one** — granting every
employee self-service must not grant them the payroll register.

**Establish whether anything in the current model would make that implication by accident:** a role
that grants by prefix, a wildcard, a hierarchy, a seed that bundles. **If nothing does, say so
explicitly** — that is a finding, not an absence, and `authorization-model.md` will rest on it.

## What NOT to do

- **Do not add a permission, a name, a seed, or a guard.** Not one line under `src/`.
- **Do not propose a naming convention.** Report what the existing one implies; the choice is the
  architect's and may be the owner's.
- **Do not assume `.self` is the shape.** It is the shape `OD-SS-0001` used to express the ruling.
  **If the codebase's existing model points at a different expression of the same ruling, that is
  exactly what this task is for.**

## Done when

- Each of the four is answered with file and line.
- **The accidental-implication question is answered yes or no**, with the mechanism named either way.
- Anything unestablished is **named as unestablished** rather than omitted.
- A result file only. **Non-gated.**

**No check-back. Report when done.**
