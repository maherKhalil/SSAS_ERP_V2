## The two invariants, each enforced twice

**Monotonic append.** The domain refuses an append at or behind the tenant's current maximum
(`SubscriptionErrors.NonMonotonicAppend`), and `UX_TenantSubscriptions_Tenant_EffectiveFromDesc` makes
two records at one instant impossible rather than merely rare. Integration proves the second against
real SQL, because two callers could each read the same maximum and both satisfy the first.

**Additive-only grants.** `RaiseLimit` refuses a value at or below the plan's cap — the loud half, naming
both numbers at the moment of the mistake. Resolution takes `max(plan, grants)` — the structural half.
`A_grant_naming_a_lower_value_cannot_lower_the_cap` constructs a lowering grant the write-time guard
cannot see and asserts the cap is unchanged: **remove the refusal entirely and that test still passes.**
