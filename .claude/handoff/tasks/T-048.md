# T-048 — Why the tenant context scaffolds a destructive migration

- **Branch:** `agent/T-048-tenant-scaffold-diagnosis` — branch from `main`
- **Module:** Platform / tooling
- **Issued:** 2026-08-26
- **Depends on:** T-047 merge (PR #92)

## Goal

**This task diagnoses. It does not fix.** The fix depends entirely on the cause, and today produced
four cases of a plausible cause accepted without being tested against the symptom — two of them mine,
one of which reached a task spec carrying the word *verified*.

You found it in T-047: scaffolding a **no-op** probe against `TenantDbContext` emits **157 operations**,
opening with `DropTable` on `AttendanceCalendarHolidays` and `AttendanceLeaveBalances`, with EF's
*"may result in the loss of data"* warning. You established it is not T-047's — stashed, re-scaffolded,
157 identical — and restored the snapshot to `HEAD`.

**Anyone scaffolding a tenant migration today and trusting the output would drop shipped module
tables.** And per your own `DEC-L-045` limit, the volume and the warning do not protect them: 157
operations reads as *a big migration, review carefully*, when it should read as *this output must be
discarded*.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] **The cause is established against the symptom, not merely made plausible.** Your
      `ComposedTenantModelSource` hypothesis — that the design-time factory composes a partial model, so
      tables of modules it does not compose read as deletions — is **a suspicion and is labelled one**.
      Confirm it or refute it, and say which by evidence rather than by argument.
- [ ] **If confirmed: show the mechanism.** Which modules the design-time path composes versus which
      the runtime Host composes, and the exact join between "not composed" and "emitted as `DropTable`".
      A count that matches is corroboration; the composition list is the evidence.
- [ ] **If refuted: say so and report what you found instead**, even if that is "cause not yet
      established". A wrong cause recorded confidently is worse than an open question — that is the
      lesson this task exists to avoid repeating.
- [ ] **Scope the blast radius.** Which contexts are affected, whether the Platform context has the
      same exposure by a different route, and whether any migration already in the repository was
      scaffolded this way. **That last one matters most** — if a committed migration carries operations
      nobody intended, this stops being a tooling hazard and becomes a data one.
- [ ] **Propose the fix without applying it**, with options where they exist and their costs named.
      **`DEC-L-045` applies to whatever you propose:** a repair that makes the scaffold quiet without
      making the model right would replace a loud defect with a silent one.
- [ ] **Nothing under `src/`, `tests/` or `docs/` is modified.** Probes removed, snapshots restored to
      `HEAD` exactly, verified rather than assumed — as you did in T-047.
