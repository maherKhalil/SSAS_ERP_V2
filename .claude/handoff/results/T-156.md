## ⚠ The two that looked live and are not — and both are the trap that retired the old guard

**`configuration` matched `TenantConfiguration.cs`.** It is `IEntityTypeConfiguration<Tenant>` — **an EF
entity-type configuration, not tenant-scoped configuration.** A guard searching the criterion's word would
have gone red on a name collision.

**`immutable-audit-store` matched six files, and every one is a comment describing what does NOT exist** —
*"if history is ever required it will be a separate append-only log"* in four HR files, and one Integration
assertion that certain tables are absent. **The only append-only structure in the product is branch history,
which is one aggregate's domain history, not a cross-cutting audit store.**

**So a word-sweep would have reported two of six as crossed. Both are false.** The concept sweep is the
instrument; the word sweep is the failure mode that produced the guard you retired.
