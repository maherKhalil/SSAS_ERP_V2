## Design constraints

- **The enforcement seam is `PermissionEndpointConventions`, and the two planes stay separate.**
  `RequirePermission` and `RequirePlatformPermission` are deliberately two methods so a caller cannot
  choose the wrong plane by passing a flag. The module gate is a sibling of that mechanism
  (`DEC-SUB-0006`), not a new authorization architecture.
- **Platform-plane routes are never gated** (`REQ-SUB-0013`). Authentication, tenant selection,
  refresh, logout, platform support and the subscription surface itself stay reachable — otherwise a
  disable locks a tenant out of the surface that would re-enable it.
- **Entitlements never travel in the access token** (`DEC-SUB-0005`, `REQ-SUB-0008`). `FP-002` excludes
  "subscription or billing information" by name under exact claim cardinality. The API contracts must
  not imply a claim.
- **`BR-SUB` numbering starts at `0001` and is contiguous.** `trace-check` will say so if it is not.
