## ⚠ A MISTAKE OF MY OWN, DURING THE PLANT

**I reverted the plant with `git checkout -- <file>`, and it discarded every uncommitted T-109 edit in that
file along with it.** Sites 1a and 1b were gone; I caught it because the restore check printed the planted
string still present, re-applied both, and re-ran.

**Nothing was lost and nothing shipped wrong** — but the lesson is exact: **`git checkout --` reverts to
HEAD, not to "before the plant".** On a file with uncommitted work the plant must be undone by its own
inverse edit. **The same family as `git rm --cached` in T-076: a git command whose scope is wider than the
intent that reached for it.**
