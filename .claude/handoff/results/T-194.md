# T-194 (item 41) — what is missing before the ERP is done

**Enumerate and rank. Nothing built, nothing fixed.** Every number was measured today by running an
instrument, not by reading a document or recalling a decision.

## The axes, declared once

| axis | question | source of truth |
|---|---|---|
| **1 — capability with no endpoint** | a feature package documents a route the application does not serve | the 15 `api-contracts.md` documents vs the routes the running host actually maps |
| **2 — endpoint with no behavioural test** | a live route that no test ever issues a request against | the running host's route table vs every non-inventory test source |
| **3 — owner decision blocking work** | a decision engineering cannot settle, and what it stops | `OWNER-DECISIONS.md`, re-read against the tree |

Axis 2 excludes route *inventories* and the contract guard deliberately: those enumerate routes without
exercising them, and counting them as coverage is how a route list becomes mistaken for a test suite.

⚠ **Axis 2's instrument over-reported until it was corrected, and the correction is part of the result.**
The first version matched route paths literally and reported 63 gaps. `DepartmentEndpointTests` declares
`const string Route = "/api/hr/departments"` and builds `$"{Route}/{id}/move"`, so the path never appears
contiguously and a *tested* route read as untested. Inlining each file's own string constants dropped HR
from 15 to 1 and the total to 42, and `departments/{}/move` — verified tested by hand — is no longer
reported. The remaining entries were then spot-checked individually.

## Axis 2 — live endpoints no test ever calls

**42 of 153 live routes.**

| module | untested routes |
|---|---|
| attendance | **25** |
| gl | 7 |
| payroll | 7 |
| platform | 2 |

⚠ **Attendance's 25 is its entire administrative surface, and it was confirmed independently of the
instrument.** `tests/API.Tests/Attendance/` holds a route inventory, a permission sweep that asserts each
route *requires* a permission without calling it, a transport test that reflects over request records, and
a self-service test that exercises `/me/*` only. **Not one HTTP request is issued against leave requests,
leave types, leave balances, calendars, periods, or records.** The handlers are well tested in
`Attendance.Tests`; what is unproven is the wiring — routing, binding, status codes, error mapping.

GL's and Payroll's seven each are money paths: journal reversals, fiscal-period closure and reopening,
account activation, run calculation, one-off payments, payslip retrieval.

## The ranked twelve

1. **#11 — the commercial plane.** Blocks *selling the product*: no plan, no price, no subscription. FP-014
   independently shows 24 documented routes with no endpoint. Two instruments, one conclusion.
2. **Attendance's 25 untested endpoints.** The largest untested surface in the product, feeding payroll
   money, and cheap to close — the host and stubs already exist.
3. **#2 — platform administration transport.** Every tenant, role and user change is manual today.
4. **FP-001's 21 unrouted rows**, 17 naming a built handler. Exposure work, not construction.
5. **#4 — self-service grants.** Caps self-service at a handful of staff.
6. **FP-003's 15 unrouted rows** — tenant lifecycle.
7. **#3 — employment type.** Blocks the first part-timer, contractor or intern.
8. **GL's 7 untested endpoints** — reversals and period closure are the ledger's correction paths.
9. **Payroll's 7** — run calculation and one-off payments are money the endpoint layer never proves.
10. **#5 — attendance bulk import.** One request per record today.
11. **FP-007's 6 unrouted rows** — department.
12. **Retire #10 from the owner's list**, and mark #1 done.

## My judgement on what comes next

**If the owner intends to sell this product in its current cycle, #11 is the only correct answer** and
nothing on this list competes: everything else improves a product that cannot yet be bought. It is also the
one item where the documentation and the code agree about the size of the hole.

**If selling is not this cycle's goal, do Attendance's endpoint tests.** It is the largest untested surface,
it is money-adjacent through payroll, the harness already exists, and — unlike every other entry here — it
is entirely within engineering's gift and needs no decision from anyone.

**What I would not do next is anything on axis 1 below FP-001.** Those are real gaps and none of them is
load-bearing this month.
