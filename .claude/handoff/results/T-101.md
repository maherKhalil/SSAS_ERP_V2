# THE DECISION I MADE INSIDE YOUR RULING, AND IT IS THE WHOLE GUARD

**"Remove the marker from every FP document title" would have deleted three titles that are not markers.**

```
FP-012-payroll/decisions-approved.md      # FP-012 — Approved decisions
FP-013-attendance/decisions-ratified.md   # FP-013 — Ratified decisions
FP-014-subscription/decisions-ratified.md # FP-014 — Ratified decisions
```

**A status word naming a DOCUMENT is not a claim about its state.** `decisions-ratified.md` is titled
*"Ratified decisions"* because that is what the document **is** — the record of which decisions were
ratified. Removing the word renames the file's subject rather than deleting a stale claim.

**`# FP-012 — Decisions (APPROVED)` was the ambiguous one**, and it is now `Approved decisions` — the same
shape as its two siblings, so the word survives where it names the document and the parenthesised form is
gone everywhere.

## And the justification is the FILENAME, not an allow-list

**A title may lead with a status word exactly when the document's own name carries it.**

```
decisions-ratified.md  ->  "Ratified decisions"   allowed, filename carries it
requirements.md        ->  "Proposed requirements"  refused, filename does not
```

**That needs no maintained exception and cannot go stale**, because the title and the filename move
together or the file is renamed. **An allow-list of three would have been the fifth thing in this loop a
person has to keep current** — which is the argument that dissolved `UnroutedFamilies` and the one I made
in T-097 against seeding an exemption list.
