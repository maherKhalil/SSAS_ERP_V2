# T-060 — `trace-check.py` is RED on 9 of 14 packages, and most of it is one renamed prefix

**Establish before changing anything. The mixed packages carry a real hazard and a blind rename
would create silent collisions.**

## What the red actually is

`py scripts/trace-check.py` → `[TRACE RED] 9 of 14 package(s) have failures`, **84 failure lines.**
Classified:

```
55  ORPHAN BR ...   cited in requirements.md / traceability-matrix.md, not defined in business-rules.md
 8  traceability-matrix.md has no parseable chain rows
 7  CONTIGUITY TS   missing identifiers in a range
 4  ORPHAN TS  ·  4 UNRESOLVED TS  ·  2 UNRESOLVED BR  ·  2 ORPHAN DEC  ·  1 CONTIGUITY DEC
```

**The 55 are not missing content.** The rules exist — under a different prefix. `FP-001`'s
`requirements.md` cites `BR-IAM-0001`; its `business-rules.md` defines **`BRULE-IAM-0001`**, same
rule, same number. The checker looks for `BR-` and finds nothing.

**A convention changed mid-project and was never back-applied:**

```
FP-001..FP-005    BRULE- only,  BR- = 0        RED
FP-006..FP-009    BOTH prefixes in one file    RED
FP-010            no business-rules.md          GREEN (the checker does not require the file)
FP-011..FP-014    BR- only                      mostly GREEN
```

## The hazard, and it is the whole reason this is not a `sed`

**In `FP-006`..`FP-009` both prefixes are present in the same file.** Before renaming anything,
establish for each of those four packages:

**Is `BRULE-XX-000N` the same rule as `BR-XX-000N`, or a different one?**

If they are the same, the rename is a merge and duplicates must be removed. **If they are different
rules that happen to share a number, a blind rename silently collapses two rules into one** — and
the checker would go green on a package that had lost a business rule. **That is the worst available
outcome here: a green that was bought by destroying content.**

**Report the answer before touching those four.** `FP-001`..`FP-005` are unambiguous — no `BR-` at
all — and may proceed once the mixed four are understood.

## The other 29 failures are not this and must not be swept in

- **8 × unparseable traceability matrix** — the table format differs from what check 5 expects.
  Establish whether the matrix is wrong or the parser is narrow. **Do not reformat nine documents to
  satisfy a parser without first checking the parser is right**; `DEC-L-060` exists because of
  exactly this kind of assumption.
- **7 × TS contiguity, 4 × ORPHAN TS, and the rest** — a missing identifier in a range is either a
  deleted scenario (declare the gap) or a lost one (write it). **Those are different remedies and the
  distinction is per-identifier.** Report the split; do not guess it.

## Why it went unnoticed

**`trace-check.py` is not in `.github/workflows/ci.yml` and not in `scripts/gate.sh`.** Nothing runs
it. That is why nine red packages have sat unremarked — not an instrument that lies, but one nobody
invokes. **Wiring it in is out of scope for this task** and becomes worth doing the moment the count
reaches zero; raising it here so the sequencing is deliberate.
