## Goal

You found these while marking the registers, and they are the fifth instance of the class we have now
named: **a ruling lands in the file that records rulings and not in the files that cite them.**

`REQ-SUB-0025` through `REQ-SUB-0028` are still marked **CONDITIONAL ON** decisions that carry rulings,
with authority **UNAUTHORED**. `OD-SUB-0015` (multi-currency, prorated), `OD-SUB-0016` (the product
issues invoices and captures payment, as qualified by `ADR-029`) and `OD-SUB-0017` (seats plus limits)
were all ruled by the owner on 2026-08-25 and are recorded in `decisions-ratified.md`.

  ruling gets dated, not silently overwritten.
